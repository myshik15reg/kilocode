# Quality Gates

Source of truth: [`../skills/quality-gates/SKILL.md`](../skills/quality-gates/SKILL.md:1).

## Summary

1. Follow the repository's required testing discipline.
2. Meet the repository's coverage policy where applicable.
3. Meet the repository's lint policy where applicable.
4. Use waivers only through an explicit documented exception flow.
